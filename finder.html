Fee-Trap Finder

Upload one credit card agreement. The agent reads the rates-and-fees box and the fine print, pulls out the real fees with their amounts, triggers, and the exact clause each comes from, then reviews and corrects its own work before showing you a plain-language report ranked by how much the fee could cost you.

Reads one document. Uses no personal data. Never tells you whether to get, keep, or close a card.
This finder runs inside Claude.

It asks Claude to read the agreement, so it needs to be opened from your Claude account. Open this artifact in claude.ai and allow it to use Claude when prompted.

Classic Banana Bread

Prep time: 15 minutes. Bake time: 60 minutes. Makes one loaf.

Ingredients:
3 ripe bananas, mashed
1/3 cup melted butter
3/4 cup sugar
1 egg, beaten
1 teaspoon vanilla extract
1 teaspoon baking soda
Pinch of salt
1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour

Instructions:
Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F. Mix the melted butter into the mashed bananas. Mix in the sugar, egg, and vanilla. Sprinkle the baking soda and salt over the mixture and stir in. Add the flour last and mix until just combined. Pour the batter into a greased loaf pan. Bake for about 60 minutes, until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean. Cool before slicing.

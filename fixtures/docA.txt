CARDHOLDER AGREEMENT

Interest Rates and Interest Charges

Annual Percentage Rate (APR) for Purchases: 24.99%. This APR will vary with the market based on the Prime Rate. Interest is charged on purchases from the transaction date unless you pay your entire statement balance by the due date each month.

Fees

Foreign Transaction Fee: All transactions processed outside of the United States (which may include internet transactions) will be charged a foreign transaction fee in the amount disclosed on your Account Opening Disclosure, as amended from time to time.

Late Payment Fee: We will charge a late payment fee of up to $40 if we do not receive your total minimum payment by the payment due date shown on your billing statement.
